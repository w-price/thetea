# The Tea visual baseline

The Tea should feel like the most fun corner of a group chat: feminine,
expressive, a little dramatic, and polished enough that players want to share a
screenshot. New screens should reuse the tokens in `static/styles.css` rather
than introduce one-off colors or shapes.

## Core palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#421538` | Primary text and high-contrast details |
| Plum | `#76245f` | Brand anchor and gradient depth |
| Pink | `#f34f9d` | Viral energy, primary actions, highlights |
| Soft pink | `#ffd7e9` | Secondary actions and friendly surfaces |
| Coral | `#ff7897` | Supporting social accents |
| Lavender | `#a962d3` | Supporting social accents and depth |
| Cream | `#fff9f4` | Cards and sheets |
| Blush paper | `#fff0f6` | Page background |

## Typography

Use a heavy rounded system stack for both display and interface copy. Headlines
are oversized, tightly spaced, and conversational. Supporting copy stays short
and direct. Avoid formal serif styling and generic corporate language.

## Shape and depth

- Cards and sheets use generous 30px-class radii.
- Buttons and inputs use 17–18px radii and comfortable touch targets.
- Primary actions use the pink-to-plum gradient.
- Shadows are pink/plum-tinted rather than gray.
- Circular avatars and soft background bubbles reinforce the party atmosphere.

## Tone guardrails

- Playful, not juvenile.
- Girly by default, without excluding anyone from playing.
- Energetic, but keep text legible and interactions obvious.
- Design for screenshots: game state, reveals, and leaderboard moments should
  look complete when cropped to a phone screen.
